# chrome-wedge

## The problem
You're testing a web app that expects a barcode scanner, like a till, a warehouse screen or a stock count, and there's no scanner on your desk. So you type codes by hand, one slow digit at a time, or fake a scan that the app quietly ignores because it doesn't look like real typing.

## How it helps
- Type a code, or drop a picture of a barcode, and it arrives in the field you clicked as real keystrokes, followed by Enter, just like a scanner.
- Reads Code 128, EAN-13 and UPC-A barcodes from images.
- Save the codes you test with most and send them with one click or a shortcut.
- Only types on sites you switch on, only into the field you clicked, and never into password fields.

## Who it's for
- Developers building point of sale, warehouse and inventory apps.
- Testers who need to scan the same codes again and again without a scanner.
