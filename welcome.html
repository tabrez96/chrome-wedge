chrome-wedge

Scans arrive as real keystrokes. Chrome will show a bar while it types.

To press keys the way a hardware scanner does, chrome-wedge uses Chrome's debugging connection. Chrome always shows this bar on that tab, and no extension can hide it.

  1. 1

    Turn it on for a site

    Open the popup on your test site and switch it on. Other sites are never touched.

  2. 2

    Click a field

    Put the cursor in the field your app reads scans from, like “Item code”.

  3. 3

    Scan

    Type a code or drop a barcode image. It's typed in, followed by Enter.

Built to stay out of the way: it only types into the field you focused (or the page itself if none is), on the tab you switched on, and never into password fields.
